Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how TU Sathi collects, uses, protects and handles information when you use our website.

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. Introduction

TU Sathi is an educational platform designed to help students access academic notes, syllabus, question papers and other educational resources.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit or interact with TU Sathi Nepal and how that information may be used.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when using certain features of the website.

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Course, semester and subject information
  • Information submitted through the contribution form
  • Messages and information submitted through contact forms

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a specific service.

3. Student Contributions

If you submit educational material through our contribution system, information such as your name, email address, course, semester, subject and uploaded document may be submitted for review.

Submitted materials may be reviewed by TU Sathi administrators before publication.

4. How We Use Information

Information provided to us may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain website features.
  • Review student contributions.
  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Improve the website and educational resources.
  • Detect abuse, spam or unauthorized activity.

5. Uploaded Documents

Documents submitted through TU Sathi may be reviewed before being made available to other students.

Users should not upload documents containing private, confidential or sensitive personal information.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

TU Sathi may use browser storage technologies, including local storage, to remember preferences such as the selected light or dark theme.

These technologies may be used to improve the user experience and do not necessarily identify you personally.

7. Third-Party Services

Some website features may rely on third-party services such as hosting providers, analytics services, payment platforms or document-viewing technologies.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through the website. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

TU Sathi is intended primarily for students and general educational use. We do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children.

10. Educational Content

TU Sathi may contain documents, notes and question papers submitted by students or collected from educational sources.

Users should respect the intellectual property rights of authors, institutions and other rights holders.

11. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. TU Sathi is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact TU Sathi through our contact page.
