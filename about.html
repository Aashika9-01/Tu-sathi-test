About TU Sathi

Built for every learner in Nepal

TU Sathi is a student-focused learning platform created to make quality academic resources easier to find, use and share. We believe learning becomes better when students learn and grow together.

◎

Our Mission

To make high-quality academic resources accessible to students by creating a simple platform where learners can discover, use and contribute useful study materials.

◉

Our Vision

To build a connected learning community where every student in Nepal has the resources and support they need to learn, improve and succeed.
